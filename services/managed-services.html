IT managed services, with one accountable team.

We take responsibility for keeping your Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Microsoft 365 and Azure estate healthy: monitoring, incidents, patching, small enhancements and release readiness, all against agreed service levels.

Talk to us What's included

Most business systems fail slowly: a flow stops running, licences drift, a release wave changes a form, nobody owns the integration. Managed services give those problems an owner.

You get a named service lead, a ticketing channel, agreed response and resolution times, and a short monthly report showing what was fixed, what changed and what we recommend next.

A good fit if

  • Your internal IT team is stretched across too many platforms
  • Your implementation partner has moved on and nobody owns the system
  • You want predictable monthly costs for support
  • You need cover outside office hours

Power Platform CoE

Microsoft 365 Administration

Backup & Disaster Recovery

What is Dataverse?

Dataverse is the secure data platform behind Dynamics 365 and Power Platform, with tables, relationships, business logic, security roles and auditing. We design data models, security and integrations so your apps share one reliable source of data.

Key capabilities

01

Data modelling

Tables, columns, relationships and choices designed for growth.

02

Security model

Business units, security roles, teams and field-level security.

03

Business logic

Business rules, plugins, custom APIs and low-code plugins.

04

Integration

Web API, virtual tables, Azure Synapse Link and Fabric link.
